## 1. The kinds

The instrument library carries one kind per instrument family, each
with its classification axes, its physics stages, its twin contract,
and its active domain (the actuation vocabulary of its world):

| Kind | Family | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| `sst-r60` | Load cells (R 60) | mass |
| `sst-r91` | Radar speed meters (R 91) | speed |
| `sst-r129` | Dimensional instruments (R 129) | dimensions |
| `sst-r144` | Continuous gas analyzers (R 144) | gas-concentration |
| `sst-sampling-line` | The extractive sampling subsystem (R 144 §2.1) | flow |

## 2. The physics stages

An indication is computed through the kind’s physics chain, and the
environment acts at each stage. The load cell’s chain is the clearest
example: mechanical deflection under load, transduction to the
electrical signal, conditioning to the indicated value. Creep accrues
under sustained load; a temperature cycle shifts the zero and the span
by a small, configurable amount, so the same load reads slightly
differently afterward — the behavior a tester sees on a real bench.

The gas analyzer’s chain works per measurand channel: each declared
component (CO, NO, NO₂) responds with its own characteristics, and the
sampling line’s transport acts on the delivered composition (delay,
conditioning) where the composite session wires the line to the
analyzer.

## 3. The twin contract

Each kind declares its twin contract: the channels it serves (its
indication shape, its environmental context, its operations) and the
baked artifact the runtime loads. The contract is baked from the
kind’s package, and the bake is freshness-gated in CI: a kind edited
without a re-bake fails the check.

## 4. The instances

An instance is a named example device built on a kind: the ACME
LC-500 load cell, the ACME RS-180 radar, the ACME MD-3xx dimensional
instrument, the ACME CGM-200 gas analyzer, and the composite CGM
system (sampling line plus analyzer in one session). Each instance
carries its coefficients, its samples (the named starting conditions:
fresh, drifted, faulted), and its console scenario vocabulary.
